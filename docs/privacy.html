Privacy Policy for Birdhouse

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Overview

Birdhouse is a synchronized audio performance app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Display Name — When joining or hosting a session, you enter a name of your choosing. This name is visible to other session participants and is automatically deleted when the session ends.

Device Identifier — The app generates a random identifier stored locally on your device. This identifier is used only to manage session participation and is never linked to your identity.

Session Data — During an active session, the room code, participant names, and audio track URLs are temporarily stored in our database. All session data is automatically deleted when the host ends the session.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal information. We do not create user accounts.

Third-Party Services

Birdhouse uses Firebase (Google) for real-time session coordination. Firebase may collect anonymized usage data subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Birdhouse does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
